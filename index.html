MilkPrint Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 17, 2026

MilkPrint ("we," "our," or "the app") is a baby care tracking application developed by Omar Gaballah. We take your privacy and your baby's privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's stored, and your rights.

The short version: Your baby's data stays on your device. We don't upload, sell, or share any health information. Period.

1. Data We Collect

MilkPrint collects the following data, entered by you:

  • Baby profile: Name, date of birth, and gender (optional)
  • Feeding logs: Timestamps, duration, breast side, bottle volume, comfort notes
  • Sleep logs: Timestamps, duration, nap/night classification, wake reasons
  • Diaper logs: Timestamps, type (wet/dirty/mixed), color, texture, amount
  • Temperature readings: Value, unit, method
  • Medication logs: Name, dose, timestamps
  • Growth measurements: Weight, length, head circumference, source
  • Milestones: Category, name, observed date
  • Doctor visits: Date, provider, visit type, notes

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Browsing history
  • Device microphone or camera input
  • Health data from HealthKit or Google Fit
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID)
  • Financial or payment information (subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store through RevenueCat — we never see your payment details)

3. How Data Is Stored

All baby care data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database (SQLite). Your data does not leave your phone unless you explicitly choose to export it (CSV or PDF export).

We do not operate cloud servers that store your health data. We do not sync your data to any remote database.

If you use the Family tier with multi-caregiver access, data is synced between connected devices using end-to-end encrypted channels. Only invited caregivers with your share code can access this data.

4. Device Permissions

MilkPrint requests only the minimum permissions necessary:

  • Push notifications: For medication reminders, wake window nudges, and caregiver updates. You can disable these at any time in your device settings.
  • Local storage: To save your data on-device.

MilkPrint will never request access to your microphone, camera, photo library, location, contacts, HealthKit, Bluetooth, or advertising tracking. This is a deliberate design choice — not an oversight.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

MilkPrint offers optional paid subscriptions (Premium and Family tiers) processed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store via RevenueCat.

  • We do not collect or store your credit card number, bank details, or billing address.
  • RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app user ID to manage your subscription status. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your device's App Store or Play Store settings.

6. Analytics

MilkPrint may collect anonymous, aggregated usage analytics (such as which screens are visited and app crash reports) to improve the app experience. This data:

  • Contains no personally identifiable information
  • Contains no baby health data
  • Cannot be linked back to you or your baby
  • Is processed through Expo's built-in analytics and crash reporting

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MilkPrint is designed for parents and caregivers to log information about their babies. The app does not collect data directly from children. All data is entered by the parent or caregiver.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The baby profile data (name and date of birth) is stored locally on the parent's device and is not transmitted to any server.

8. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible to you in the app at all times.
  • Export: You can export all your data as CSV or PDF from Settings at any time.
  • Delete: You can delete all data from Settings → Delete Data. This permanently removes all data from your device. Since we don't store data on our servers, deletion is complete and irreversible.
  • Portability: CSV export allows you to take your data to any other service.

9. GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access, rectification, and erasure of your data
  • Right to data portability (via CSV export)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Legal basis for processing: Your data is processed based on your consent (you choose to enter it) and for the performance of the service you requested.

10. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • We do not sell your personal information. We never have and never will.
  • You have the right to know what data we collect (listed above in Section 1).
  • You have the right to delete your data (via Settings → Delete Data).
  • You have the right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

11. Third-Party Services

MilkPrint uses the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Subscription management. Receives a pseudonymous user ID only. Privacy policy
  • Expo / EAS: App build and update delivery. May receive anonymous crash reports. Privacy policy
  • IP-based weather: An approximate weather reading based on your IP address region (city-level). No GPS coordinates are ever collected or transmitted.

12. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's own security measures (passcode, biometric lock, encryption). We recommend keeping your device's security features enabled.

For Family tier users, caregiver data sync uses encrypted connections. Share codes expire and can be revoked at any time.

13. Medical Disclaimer

MilkPrint is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. The information, suggestions, and typical ranges shown in the app are based on published pediatric guidelines and are for general awareness only. Always consult your pediatrician or healthcare provider for medical concerns about your baby's health, growth, feeding, or development.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent update.
